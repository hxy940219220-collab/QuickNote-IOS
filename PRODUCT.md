# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Mac users who need to capture thoughts without leaving their current workflow. They primarily invoke QuickNote from the keyboard, type or import selected text, briefly organize the result, and return to the previous app.

## Product Purpose

QuickNote is a fast, local-first Mac note utility. Success means the note surface appears immediately, editing feels native and predictable, content is saved safely, and optional AI actions never slow down basic capture.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, native, dependable. The interface should feel familiar to users of first-party macOS tools while remaining lighter and faster than a full knowledge-management app.

## Anti-references

Avoid crowded toolbars, ambiguous icons, oversized empty areas, stacked modal dialogs, ornamental glass effects, stiff or theatrical motion, and controls that require precise targeting for common actions.

## Design Principles

1. Capture first: the shortest path to writing always wins.
2. Earn familiarity: use standard macOS behaviors and controls unless a custom interaction is materially faster.
3. Reveal depth progressively: advanced formatting and AI tools stay available without competing with the editor.
4. Make state obvious: selection, saving, locking, permissions, and destructive actions must be understandable at a glance.
5. Preserve trust: local data, recoverable edits, and predictable window behavior outrank decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support keyboard-first operation, clear accessibility labels, sufficient contrast, forgiving hit targets, and macOS Reduce Motion. Never rely on color alone to communicate state, and keep core capture usable when optional permissions are unavailable.
